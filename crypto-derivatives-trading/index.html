PERPETUALS / MARGIN / LONG & SHORT

What is crypto derivatives trading?

Crypto derivatives are contracts that reference an underlying market. They can provide long or short exposure and may use collateral and leverage. Perpetual contracts are one of the most common forms in crypto and do not have a fixed expiry date.

DERIVATIVE BASICS

The contract tracks a market, but the position has its own mechanics.

Derivative traders should understand the referenced market as well as the contract's collateral, leverage, funding, liquidation and execution rules. Those mechanics can materially affect the outcome.

01 / UNDERLYING

Referenced market

The derivative derives value from another market or asset price.

02 / DIRECTION

Long or short exposure

Contracts can allow directional positions without a conventional spot purchase.

03 / COLLATERAL

Margin supports the position

Eligible collateral may be required before a leveraged derivative can be opened.

04 / LEVERAGE

Exposure can be amplified

Leverage increases both potential gains and potential losses.

05 / FUNDING

Holding cost can vary

Perpetual markets may use recurring funding payments.

06 / LIQUIDATION

Forced closure is possible

Positions can be liquidated when margin requirements are no longer met.

SPOT VS DERIVATIVE

The exposure model is different.

SPOT

Exchange the underlying asset.

ASSETDirect market transaction

A spot trade exchanges one asset for another.

LEVERAGENot inherent

Plain spot trading does not inherently require leverage.

EXPIRYNo contract expiry

The asset position itself is not a derivative contract.

DERIVATIVE

Trade a contract referencing the market.

EXPOSURELong or short

The contract can create directional market exposure.

MARGINCollateral may be required

Leveraged contracts use margin under the venue's rules.

RISKLiquidation can apply

Losses can trigger forced closure when margin is insufficient.

DERIVATIVES FAQ

Quick answers.

What are crypto derivatives?+

They are contracts whose value references an underlying crypto market.

Are perpetuals derivatives?+

Yes. Perpetual contracts are derivatives without a fixed expiry date.

Can derivatives be leveraged?+

Yes. Supported markets may use leverage, which magnifies gains and losses and can create liquidation risk.
