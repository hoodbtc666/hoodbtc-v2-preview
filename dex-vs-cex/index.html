DEX vs CEX: what is the difference?

A decentralized exchange model generally uses blockchain-connected infrastructure and wallet authorization. A centralized exchange model generally uses an account operated by a company that manages order access and often custody. Neither model removes risk—the risks are simply distributed differently.

CORE COMPARISON

Two different ways to access crypto markets.

DEX

Wallet-connected market access.

ACCESSConnect a wallet

Users typically authorize interactions through a supported wallet rather than a traditional username/password exchange account.

CUSTODYOften non-custodial or protocol-based

The interface generally does not require handing private keys to the frontend, although protocol collateral and settlement mechanics can still exist.

RISKWallet and protocol risk

Smart contracts, wallets, networks, liquidity and transaction approvals become central concerns.

CEX

Operator-managed exchange access.

ACCESSExchange account

Users generally create an account with the centralized operator and access markets through its internal systems.

CUSTODYOften operator custody

Deposited assets may be controlled by the exchange until withdrawn, depending on the service.

RISKCounterparty and account risk

Users rely on the operator's security, solvency, withdrawal systems, policies and geographic availability.

WHAT TO COMPARE

Choose based on the complete workflow, not one slogan.

Trading fees matter, but they are only one part of the decision. Consider custody, liquidity, market depth, execution, withdrawals, supported assets, local availability and the risk controls you understand.

01 / CUSTODY

Who controls the assets?

Understand whether funds stay in your wallet, move into protocol-controlled collateral or sit in an exchange-controlled account.

02 / LIQUIDITY

How deep is the market?

Low liquidity can increase slippage and make large orders harder to execute efficiently.

03 / EXECUTION

How are orders matched?

Different venues use orderbooks, automated market makers or connected matching infrastructure.

04 / WITHDRAWAL

How do assets leave?

Review withdrawal mechanics, network costs, settlement time and any platform restrictions.

05 / SECURITY

What can go wrong?

DEX users manage wallet and protocol security; CEX users also accept operator and account risk.

06 / LEGAL ACCESS

Where is the service available?

Market and geographic restrictions can differ across both centralized and decentralized services.

DEX VS CEX FAQ

Quick answers.

What is the main difference between a DEX and CEX?+

A DEX generally uses wallet-connected decentralized infrastructure, while a CEX generally uses an exchange account managed by a centralized operator.

Is a DEX automatically safer?+

No. The risk model is different. Wallet approvals, smart contracts, protocols, networks and liquidity can all introduce risk.

Does a CEX always hold my assets?+

Many centralized exchanges use custodial deposit accounts, but exact custody and product structures vary by service.
