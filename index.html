THE PROBLEM

Onchain trading is powerful. The experience is still fragmented.

Traders can face disconnected wallets, networks, liquidity venues, interfaces, position views, and execution workflows. HOODBTC is building a cleaner access layer that brings the trading journey into one focused experience.

01 / ACCESS

Wallet-first access

Connect a supported wallet and move directly into the trading experience without creating another conventional username-and-password exchange account.

02 / CONTROL

Non-custodial by design

HOODBTC is designed as an interface rather than a traditional custodian. Users interact through their own wallet and retain wallet-level control.

03 / LIQUIDITY

Shared liquidity infrastructure

The interface connects to decentralized trading infrastructure designed to provide market connectivity, liquidity, matching, settlement, and execution services.

04 / SIMPLICITY

Less fragmentation

Market discovery, wallet access, positions, orders, and execution context are organized into a single trader-focused experience.

05 / TRANSPARENCY

Clear infrastructure boundaries

HOODBTC focuses on the product and user experience while underlying decentralized infrastructure can provide core trading services.

06 / ACCESSIBILITY

Internet-native market access

HOODBTC is designed for broad onchain access across supported environments, subject to infrastructure availability and applicable geographic restrictions.

Wallet access, KYC and geographic availability

HOODBTC does not use a traditional account-opening flow on this website. Identity verification, wallet support, markets and geographic availability may still depend on the underlying infrastructure, networks and applicable law. Access is not guaranteed in every jurisdiction.
