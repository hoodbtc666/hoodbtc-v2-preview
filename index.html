WHY HOODBTC

Trading should feel connected — not fragmented.

HOODBTC is designed to simplify the journey between a trader, a wallet, and supported onchain markets. Instead of forcing users through disconnected tools and interfaces, HOODBTC brings the experience into one focused access layer.

01 / ACCESS

Wallet-first entry

Open the platform, connect a supported wallet, and move directly into the trading experience without creating another conventional exchange account.

02 / CONTROL

Non-custodial by design

HOODBTC is built as an interface, not a traditional custodian. Your supported wallet remains the access and authorization layer.

03 / LIQUIDITY

Shared market liquidity

HOODBTC connects to decentralized trading infrastructure designed to support liquidity, matching, settlement, and execution across supported markets.

04 / EXPERIENCE

One focused interface

Market discovery, wallet access, orders, positions, and execution context are organized around the trader instead of scattered across products.

05 / TRANSPARENCY

Clear infrastructure boundaries

HOODBTC focuses on the product experience while underlying infrastructure handles core decentralized trading functions.

06 / ACCESSIBILITY

Internet-native access

The product is designed for broad onchain accessibility wherever supported infrastructure and applicable local requirements permit.

Access, KYC and geographic availability

FAQ

Know what you are connecting to.

What is HOODBTC?+

HOODBTC is a decentralized trading interface focused on wallet-first access, shared liquidity, professional market access, and non-custodial control.

Does HOODBTC hold my funds?+

HOODBTC is designed as a non-custodial interface. Users connect supported wallets and authorize wallet interactions themselves. Underlying infrastructure may have its own technical flow and requirements.

How do I connect a wallet?+

Open Trade Now, select Connect Wallet, choose a supported wallet, review the wallet prompt, approve the connection, then verify your address and network before trading.

Does HOODBTC require KYC?+

The HOODBTC website does not present a traditional account-opening flow. Identity verification requirements can still depend on underlying infrastructure, product, location, and applicable law.

Can I trade from any country?+

HOODBTC is designed for broad onchain access, but availability is subject to supported infrastructure and applicable geographic restrictions. Access is not guaranteed in every jurisdiction.

What should I verify before trading?+

Verify the official domain, connected wallet address, network, market, available balance, order type, leverage if applicable, and all wallet approval details.
