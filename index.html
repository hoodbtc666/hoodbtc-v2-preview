WHY HOODBTC

What problem is HOODBTC solving?

Crypto trading is often fragmented across exchanges, wallets, networks, and interfaces. HOODBTC is designed to reduce that friction by giving traders one focused, wallet-connected interface for accessing supported onchain markets.

01 / ACCESS

No traditional account signup

HOODBTC uses wallet-first access instead of asking users to create a traditional username-and-password trading account.

02 / CONTROL

Keep control of your wallet

The experience is non-custodial by design. Users connect their own wallet rather than depositing assets into a HOODBTC-controlled account.

03 / LIQUIDITY

One interface, shared liquidity

HOODBTC connects the trader-facing experience to decentralized infrastructure that can support liquidity, market connectivity, matching, and settlement.

04 / GLOBAL

Built for global onchain access

The platform is internet-native and designed for broad access wherever the service, underlying infrastructure, and applicable laws permit.

About KYC and geographic access

HOODBTC itself does not present a traditional account-opening flow on this site. However, availability, identity-verification requirements, blocked jurisdictions, wallet support, and market access can depend on the underlying infrastructure provider, network, and local law. We do not promise “no KYC everywhere” or unrestricted access from every country.
