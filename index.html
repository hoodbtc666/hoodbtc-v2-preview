FAQ

Know what you are connecting to.

What is HOODBTC?+

HOODBTC is a decentralized trading interface focused on wallet-first access, shared liquidity, professional market access, and non-custodial control.

Does HOODBTC hold my funds?+

HOODBTC is designed as a non-custodial interface. Users connect supported wallets and authorize wallet interactions themselves. Underlying infrastructure may have its own technical flow and requirements.

How do I connect a wallet?+

Open Trade Now, select Connect Wallet, choose a supported wallet, review the wallet prompt, approve the connection, then verify your address and network before trading.

Does HOODBTC require KYC?+

The HOODBTC website does not present a traditional account-opening flow. Identity verification requirements can still depend on underlying infrastructure, product, location, and applicable law.

Can I trade from any country?+

HOODBTC is designed for broad onchain access, but availability is subject to supported infrastructure and applicable geographic restrictions. Access is not guaranteed in every jurisdiction.

What should I verify before trading?+

Verify the official domain, connected wallet address, network, market, available balance, order type, leverage if applicable, and all wallet approval details.

Why use HOODBTC instead of a traditional exchange interface?+

HOODBTC is designed around wallet-first, non-custodial access and a focused onchain trading experience. The tradeoff is that decentralized market access still carries wallet, smart-contract, market, and infrastructure risks that users should understand.

Is HOODBTC finished?+

The trading access is live while the HOODBTC product experience continues to be refined. Features should only be treated as available when they are visible and usable on the live platform.
