EXPOSURE / COLLATERAL / LEVERAGE

Understand position size before opening a crypto trade.

Position size is the amount of market exposure a trade creates. It should be reviewed together with collateral, leverage, volatility and liquidation risk rather than treated as an isolated number.

POSITION SIZE BASICS

A trade can be too large even when the direction is right.

Market direction, trade size and leverage all contribute to outcome. Larger exposure creates larger gains and losses for the same percentage market move, while leverage can increase that exposure relative to the collateral available.

POSITION SIZE FAQ

Quick answers.

What is position size?+

It is the total market exposure created by a trade.

Why does size matter?+

Larger exposure produces larger gains or losses for the same percentage move.

Should leverage be considered separately?+

No. Position size, leverage and collateral should be considered together.
