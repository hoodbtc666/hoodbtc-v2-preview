WALLET-FIRST DECENTRALIZED TRADING

Trade through a non-custodial DEX interface.

HOODBTC Trade is designed around wallet-first access. The user initiates and authorizes supported interactions from a connected wallet rather than handing a seed phrase or private key to the HOODBTC website. Connected infrastructure can still be involved in market access, liquidity, matching and settlement.

HOW IT WORKS

Your wallet is the authorization layer.

Non-custodial does not mean every market action happens with no intermediate protocol state. Some trading systems may require collateral, account preparation, settlement or other wallet-authorized steps. The important distinction is that users should understand what they are approving and never disclose wallet secrets.

NON-CUSTODIAL DEX FAQ

Quick answers.

What does non-custodial DEX mean?+

It means the trading interface uses wallet-based authorization rather than requiring the frontend to take possession of your private keys.

Do I ever share my seed phrase?+

No. Never send a seed phrase or private key to a person, support account or website form.

Is non-custodial trading risk-free?+

No. Market, wallet, smart-contract, network, liquidity and leverage risks can still apply.
