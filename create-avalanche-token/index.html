AVALANCHE TOKEN FAQ

Questions before deployment.

Can I create liquidity at the same time as the token?+

Token deployment and liquidity are separate actions. Creation does not automatically create a pool, price, buyers or listing.

Does HOODBTC need my private key?+

No. Use your wallet to approve the transaction; do not send wallet secrets to another person.

What should I verify after deployment?+

Verify the C-Chain contract address, transaction, supply and any remaining contract controls.
