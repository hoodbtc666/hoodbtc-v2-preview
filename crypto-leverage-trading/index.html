MARGIN / POSITION SIZE / LIQUIDATION

Understand crypto leverage before using it.

Leverage can increase exposure beyond the collateral committed to a position. That can make capital more efficient for experienced traders, but it also means adverse price moves can create losses faster and may trigger liquidation.

CORE CONCEPTS

Read the full position, not only the leverage number.

Leverage, collateral, position size and liquidation level work together. The same leverage multiple can represent very different risk depending on position size, volatility and the amount of available margin.

01 / COLLATERAL

Margin supports the trade

Eligible collateral provides the equity that supports a leveraged position.

02 / EXPOSURE

Leverage increases exposure

A leveraged position can be larger than the collateral backing it.

03 / P&L

Losses can accelerate

Because exposure is larger, percentage moves in the market can have a larger effect on account equity.

04 / LIQUIDATION

Positions can be closed automatically

If margin falls below required levels, the market may liquidate all or part of the position.

05 / VOLATILITY

Fast markets matter

Sharp price moves can reduce the time available to react, especially at higher leverage.

06 / REVIEW

Verify every order

Check market, side, size, leverage and displayed risk information before submission.

LEVERAGE FAQ

Quick answers.

What is leverage in crypto trading?+

Leverage increases market exposure relative to collateral, magnifying both potential gains and potential losses.

Can leverage cause liquidation?+

Yes. A position can be liquidated if losses reduce margin below the market's required level.

Does higher leverage mean higher risk?+

Generally, higher leverage leaves less room for adverse price movement before margin becomes stressed.
