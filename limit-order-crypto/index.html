How does a crypto limit order work?

A limit order lets a trader specify a price condition instead of simply accepting the best available price immediately. The trade only executes when compatible liquidity is available at the limit price or better, subject to the venue's rules.

LIMIT ORDER BASICS

Price control does not guarantee execution.

A limit order can help define an acceptable execution price, but the market must still provide compatible liquidity. Fast markets can move away from the order, and large orders may fill only partially.

LIMIT ORDER FAQ

Quick answers.

What is a limit order?+

A limit order sets a price condition and only executes when compatible liquidity is available at that price or better under the venue's rules.

Can a limit order remain unfilled?+

Yes. It may remain open if the market never reaches the price or if available liquidity is insufficient.

Can it fill partially?+

Yes. Partial fills can occur when only part of the requested size can execute at eligible prices.
