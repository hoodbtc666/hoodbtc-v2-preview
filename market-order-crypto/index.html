IMMEDIATE EXECUTION / LIQUIDITY / SLIPPAGE

How does a crypto market order work?

A market order generally prioritizes immediate execution against the best available liquidity. It does not guarantee one exact execution price, so market depth, spread, volatility and order size all matter.

MARKET ORDER BASICS

Fast execution can involve price uncertainty.

A market order interacts with the liquidity that is available when it reaches the market. If the requested size is larger than the best price level, execution can continue across additional levels and change the average fill price.

MARKET ORDER FAQ

Quick answers.

What is a market order?+

A market order seeks execution against currently available liquidity rather than waiting for a specific limit price.

Why can slippage happen?+

Price can move while the order is being processed, or the order may consume several levels of available liquidity.

Does a market order guarantee one price?+

No. The average fill can differ from the visible price before submission.
