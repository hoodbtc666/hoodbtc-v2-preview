LIQUIDATION RISK

MARGIN / LEVERAGE / FORCED CLOSURE

Crypto liquidation explained.

Liquidation can occur when a leveraged position no longer has enough margin to satisfy the market's requirements. It is one of the most important risks to understand before trading perpetuals or other leveraged crypto products.

WHY LIQUIDATION HAPPENS

Leverage reduces the room for adverse price movement.

The exact formula varies by venue and market. In general, a position becomes vulnerable when losses reduce account or position equity relative to the maintenance margin requirement.

01 / ENTRY

Position opens with collateral

A leveraged trade uses margin to support a larger market exposure.

02 / MOVE

Market moves against the trade

Adverse price movement reduces unrealized P&L and available equity.

03 / MARGIN

Margin becomes stressed

As losses grow, the distance to the market's maintenance requirement can shrink.

04 / THRESHOLD

Liquidation conditions are reached

The venue may begin reducing or closing the position under its rules.

05 / VOLATILITY

Fast moves can accelerate risk

Sudden price changes can move a position toward liquidation quickly.

06 / REVIEW

Displayed estimates can change

Liquidation information may move as margin, funding, fees and market prices change.

LIQUIDATION FAQ

Quick answers.

What is liquidation?+

It is a forced reduction or closure of a leveraged position when margin no longer meets the market's requirements.

Can liquidation happen quickly?+

Yes. High leverage and volatile markets can reduce the distance to liquidation quickly.

Is the displayed liquidation price guaranteed?+

No. Risk information can change as market price, fees, funding and margin conditions change.
