FUNDING RATE GUIDE

PERPETUALS / HOLDING COST / MARKET BALANCE

Crypto funding rates explained.

Many perpetual markets use recurring funding payments to help keep contract prices close to the underlying market. Funding is not the same as a trading fee, and it can change over time as market positioning and pricing conditions change.

HOW FUNDING WORKS

Funding connects position direction with recurring market payments.

Exact formulas, intervals and settlement rules vary. Traders should use the live market information as the source of truth rather than assuming that a funding rate remains constant.

01 / RATE

The rate can change

Funding is typically calculated from market conditions and can move as pricing and positioning change.

02 / DIRECTION

Longs or shorts may pay

Depending on the sign of funding, one side of the market may pay the other.

03 / INTERVAL

Payments recur

Many perpetual markets apply funding on a recurring schedule defined by the venue.

04 / HOLD TIME

Duration matters

A position held through multiple funding intervals can accumulate payments or receipts.

05 / P&L

Funding affects net results

Trading P&L should be considered together with funding, fees and other position costs.

FUNDING FAQ

Quick answers.

What is a funding rate?+

It is a recurring payment mechanism used by many perpetual markets to help align contract and underlying prices.

Is funding always positive?+

No. Funding can be positive or negative depending on market conditions and the venue's formula.

Is funding the same as a trading fee?+

No. Funding and execution fees are separate concepts.

CHECK LIVE CONDITIONS

Funding can change while the trade is open.
