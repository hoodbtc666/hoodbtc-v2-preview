UNDERSTAND THE MODEL

What makes decentralized trading different?

Decentralized trading shifts more responsibility to the user. The wallet becomes the access and authorization layer, while connected infrastructure can support liquidity, matching, execution and settlement for supported markets.

DECENTRALIZED TRADING FAQ

Questions before trading.

What is decentralized crypto trading?+

It generally means accessing blockchain-connected market infrastructure through wallet-based interactions instead of relying only on a traditional centralized exchange account.

Does non-custodial trading remove trading risk?+

No. Market, liquidation, smart-contract, network and infrastructure risks can still apply.

Does HOODBTC need my seed phrase?+

No. Never send a seed phrase or private key to another person to access HOODBTC Trade.

Can decentralized markets use leverage?+

Some supported markets may involve derivatives or leverage. Review the specific market and liquidation mechanics before trading.
