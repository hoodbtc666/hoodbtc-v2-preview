COMMUNITY SECURITY CHECK

Verify first. Connect second.

Crypto communities attract impersonation attempts. A familiar logo or username is not enough proof by itself.

BEFORE CONNECTING

Check the destination.

DOMAINRead the full URL

Make sure the domain is an official HOODBTC destination before connecting a wallet.

WALLETRead every prompt

Check the network, transaction details, token amounts and permissions before approving.

LINKSUse this page as a reference

When in doubt, return to hoodbtc.com/community/ and open the official destination from here.

NEVER SHARE

Keep wallet secrets private.

SEED PHRASENever enter a recovery phrase into a community message

A seed phrase gives control of a wallet. Keep it offline and private.

PRIVATE KEYNever send a private key

HOODBTC product access does not require sending a private key to another person.

DMsTreat unsolicited messages as unverified

Impersonators can copy names, avatars and logos. Verify independently before following instructions or opening links.

COMMUNITY QUESTIONS

Quick answers about official HOODBTC access.

What is the official HOODBTC website?+

The main ecosystem website is hoodbtc.com. HOODBTC Trade is available at trade.hoodbtc.com and Token Factory is available at tokenfactory.hoodbtc.com.

Where is the official HOODBTC Telegram community?+

The community link published on this page points to t.me/hoodbtc2.

Should I trust a HOODBTC link sent in a direct message?+

Do not rely on a logo or display name alone. Compare the destination with the official links published on hoodbtc.com/community/ before connecting a wallet or approving anything.

Will I ever need to share my seed phrase to use HOODBTC?+

No product interaction should require sending a seed phrase or private key to another person. Wallet approvals happen through the user's own wallet interface.
