LONG VS SHORT

DIRECTION / EXPOSURE / RISK

Long vs short crypto positions.

Long and short describe the direction of market exposure. A long position generally benefits from rising prices, while a short position generally benefits from falling prices. Both can lose money, and leverage can magnify those losses.

CORE DIFFERENCE

Direction changes how price movement affects the position.

LONG

Exposure to rising prices.

UPRising market can help

A long position generally gains when the referenced market rises.

DOWNFalling market can hurt

Losses grow as price moves against the long position.

RISKLeverage increases sensitivity

Leveraged longs can be liquidated during adverse moves.

SHORT

Exposure to falling prices.

DOWNFalling market can help

A short position generally gains when the referenced market falls.

UPRising market can hurt

Losses grow when price rises against the short.

RISKUpside moves can be violent

Short squeezes and rapid rallies can create fast losses.

BEFORE ENTRY

Direction is only one part of the trade.

Traders also need to review position size, leverage, collateral, funding, order type and liquidation information. A correct market direction does not guarantee a profitable result.

LONG VS SHORT FAQ

Quick answers.

What does long mean?+

A long position generally benefits from rising prices and loses when prices fall.

What does short mean?+

A short position generally benefits from falling prices and loses when prices rise.

Can both directions be liquidated?+

Yes. Leveraged long and short positions can both be liquidated if margin requirements are no longer met.
