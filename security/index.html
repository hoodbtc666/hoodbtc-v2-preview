WALLET SAFETY

Your wallet approval is a security boundary.

Onchain actions can be irreversible. Read every wallet prompt and independently verify the network, destination, token amounts and permissions before approval.

SEED PHRASE

Never share a recovery phrase.

HOODBTC does not require you to send a seed phrase or private key to another person. Anyone asking for one should be treated as untrusted.

APPROVALS

Read before you sign.

Confirm the exact action shown by your wallet. A familiar brand name in a message or browser tab does not replace checking the transaction itself.

DMs

Do not trust unsolicited support messages.

Impersonators can copy names, avatars and logos. Return to hoodbtc.com/community/ and use the published official links instead.

PUBLIC BLOCKCHAINS

Assume onchain activity is visible.

Wallet addresses and transactions on public networks can be observable by third parties. Do not treat a public wallet address as private information.

WEBSITE PROTECTIONS

Defense in depth for the public HOODBTC website.

The main website deploys browser and transport protections intended to reduce common web risks. These controls improve resilience but cannot guarantee that any internet-connected system will never be compromised.

TRANSPORT

HTTPS enforcement

Strict Transport Security is configured to keep supported browsers on encrypted HTTPS connections.

CONTENT

Content Security Policy

A restrictive Content Security Policy limits where scripts, styles, fonts, images and connections may load from.

EMBEDDING

Frame blocking

Frame restrictions are configured to reduce clickjacking exposure on the main website.

BROWSER PERMISSIONS

Reduced unnecessary access

Camera, microphone, geolocation and payment permissions are disabled by default on the main website through policy headers.

RESPONSIBLE DISCLOSURE

Report security issues without putting users at risk.

If you believe you found a vulnerability affecting hoodbtc.com or an official HOODBTC destination, use the official contact route below and provide enough technical detail for the issue to be reproduced and assessed.

What to include

  • The affected URL, product or component.
  • Clear reproduction steps and expected versus observed behavior.
  • The likely security impact and any safe proof of concept.
  • Your preferred contact method for follow-up.

What not to do

  • Do not access, copy or modify data that is not yours.
  • Do not move user funds, attempt phishing or ask users for credentials.
  • Do not disrupt availability, perform destructive testing or publish exploit details before there is time to investigate.
  • Do not assume a reward is available. No bounty or payment is promised unless separate bounty terms are published.

SECURITY BOUNDARIES

Know what this page does — and does not — promise.

HOODBTC can publish verified destinations, safer-use guidance and security controls, but users remain responsible for securing their own devices, wallets, recovery material and approvals. Third-party wallets, blockchain networks, protocols and infrastructure may have separate security models, terms and risks.

Security information may change as products and infrastructure evolve. Material updates will be reflected on this page and in the published security.txt file.
