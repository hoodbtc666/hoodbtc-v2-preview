What is a perpetual DEX?

A perpetual DEX combines wallet-connected decentralized market access with perpetual derivative contracts. Traders can take long or short exposure in supported markets while remaining responsible for collateral, leverage, funding and liquidation risk.

PERPETUAL DEX BASICS

Decentralized access does not remove derivatives risk.

Wallet-connected access changes the account and custody model, but perpetual trading still involves market risk, collateral requirements, funding mechanics, execution risk and possible liquidation.

Wallet-connected access

No fixed expiry

PERPETUAL DEX FAQ

Quick answers.

What is a perpetual DEX?+

It is a wallet-connected decentralized trading venue or interface for perpetual derivative markets.

Can perpetual DEX trading use leverage?+

Supported perpetual markets can involve leverage and margin, which can magnify losses.

Can positions be liquidated?+

Yes. Leveraged positions may be liquidated when margin requirements are no longer met.
