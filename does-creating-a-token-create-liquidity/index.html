TOKEN + LIQUIDITY GUIDE

DEPLOYMENT ≠ MARKET LAUNCH

Does creating a token automatically create liquidity?

No. Creating a token puts an asset or contract onchain. It does not automatically create a trading pair, deposit capital into a liquidity pool, attract buyers or establish a market price. Those are separate steps with separate risks.

FOUR DIFFERENT THINGS

Token, market, liquidity and price are not the same thing.

These concepts are often mixed together. Separating them helps creators understand what a token factory actually does and what still needs to happen before a token can trade in a meaningful market.

01 / TOKEN

The onchain asset

Deployment creates the token mint or contract and establishes its technical parameters.

02 / MARKET

A place to trade

A market requires a compatible venue, pair or orderbook where participants can exchange assets.

03 / LIQUIDITY

Assets available for trading

Liquidity comes from capital or orders available to execute trades; it is not created simply by deploying a token.

04 / PRICE

The result of market interaction

Price discovery requires buyers and sellers or an automated market mechanism with supplied assets.

05 / LISTING

Venue support is separate

A token creator does not guarantee automatic listing on a DEX, CEX or HOODBTC Trade.

06 / RISK

Liquidity can be lost

Providing liquidity can expose capital to smart-contract, market, price and impermanent-loss risks depending on the venue.

LIQUIDITY FAQ

Quick answers.

Does creating a token automatically create liquidity?+

No. Liquidity requires a separate market setup and assets or orders available for trading.

Does a token automatically have a price?+

No. Price requires a market where supply and demand can interact.

Will a Token Factory token automatically appear on HOODBTC Trade?+

No. HOODBTC Token Factory and HOODBTC Trade are separate products; token creation does not create an automatic market listing.
