DEX TRADING QUESTIONS

Useful answers before trading onchain.

What is a DEX?+

A DEX, or decentralized exchange, is a trading system that uses blockchain-connected infrastructure and wallet-based interactions rather than relying only on the traditional centralized exchange account model.

Is HOODBTC a non-custodial DEX interface?+

HOODBTC is designed as a wallet-first, non-custodial trading interface. Connected infrastructure may support core market functions such as liquidity, matching and settlement.

Why might a trading balance or collateral step appear?+

Some supported markets require eligible collateral or a wallet-authorized funding step before an order can be opened. The exact flow depends on the market, network and infrastructure.

What is the difference between a market and limit order?+

A market order generally prioritizes immediate execution at available prices. A limit order sets a price condition and may remain unfilled if the market never reaches it or liquidity is unavailable.

Can DEX trading involve leverage?+

Some supported decentralized markets may involve derivatives or leverage. Leverage can significantly increase risk and losses, so users should verify the specific market and parameters before trading.

Can I trade 24/7?+

Many onchain markets operate around the clock, but actual availability depends on the specific market, network, infrastructure and geographic restrictions.
