DEX TRADING QUESTIONS

Useful answers before trading onchain.

What is a DEX?+

A DEX, or decentralized exchange, is a trading system that uses blockchain-connected infrastructure and wallet-based interactions rather than the traditional centralized exchange account model.

Is HOODBTC a non-custodial DEX interface?+

HOODBTC is designed as a wallet-first, non-custodial trading interface. Underlying infrastructure providers may support core market functions such as liquidity, matching and settlement.

Do I need to deposit funds into HOODBTC first?+

The website is designed around wallet-connected access rather than a conventional exchange deposit account. The exact technical flow can depend on the supported market and underlying infrastructure.

Can DEX trading involve leverage?+

Some supported decentralized markets may involve derivatives or leverage. Leverage can significantly increase risk and losses, so users should verify the specific market and parameters before trading.

Can I trade 24/7?+

Many onchain markets operate around the clock, but actual availability depends on the specific market, network, infrastructure and geographic restrictions.
