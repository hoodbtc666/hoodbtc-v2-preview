ANTI-IMPERSONATION

Support should never require your wallet secrets.

  • Never share a seed phrase, recovery phrase or private key.
  • Do not send funds to a person claiming they need payment to “unlock” your wallet.
  • Do not install remote-access software because a stranger claims to be HOODBTC support.
  • Do not trust a display name, logo or copied profile image as proof of identity.
  • When uncertain, stop and return to hoodbtc.com/community/ to verify the official destination.
Security note: HOODBTC cannot reverse a blockchain transaction simply because it was approved by mistake. Verify first and approve only what you understand.
