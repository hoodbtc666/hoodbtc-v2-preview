How does an order book DEX work?

An order book organizes available buy and sell interest by price. Traders can review bids, asks and market depth before choosing an order type. In a wallet-first DEX model, the interface connects the trader to decentralized market infrastructure without relying only on a conventional centralized exchange account.

ORDER BOOK BASICS

Depth shows more than a single last-traded price.

An order book displays buy and sell interest across price levels. The available quantity near the current market can influence spread, slippage and the ability to execute larger orders efficiently.

01 / BIDS

Buy interest

Bids represent available or resting interest to buy at specified prices.

02 / ASKS

Sell interest

Asks represent available or resting interest to sell at specified prices.

03 / SPREAD

Difference between sides

The bid-ask spread is the gap between the best available buy and sell prices.

04 / DEPTH

Liquidity across levels

Depth shows how much quantity is available beyond the top price.

05 / LIMIT

Resting orders

Limit orders can wait in the market until compatible execution becomes available.

06 / MARKET

Immediate execution

Market orders consume available liquidity and can cross multiple price levels.

ORDER BOOK FAQ

Quick answers.

What is an order book DEX?+

It is a wallet-connected decentralized trading interface or venue that organizes buy and sell interest by price through order-book infrastructure.

What is market depth?+

Market depth is the amount of available buy and sell liquidity across different price levels.

Why does depth matter for large orders?+

If available liquidity is thin, a large order may execute across several price levels and experience more slippage.
