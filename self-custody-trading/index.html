WHAT SELF-CUSTODY MEANS

Control of keys comes with responsibility.

Self-custody is about control of wallet credentials and approvals. It does not remove market, smart-contract, network, leverage or infrastructure risk. Good self-custody practice means protecting keys and reading every action before approval.

01 / KEYS

Your keys remain private

Do not send a private key or recovery phrase to HOODBTC, support staff or another person.

02 / DOMAIN

Verify the official destination

Use trade.hoodbtc.com and return to hoodbtc.com if you are unsure about a link.

03 / APPROVAL

Read wallet prompts

Check the network, destination and requested action before authorizing anything.

04 / ORDER

Review trade parameters

Confirm market, side, size, order type and leverage where applicable.

05 / POSITION

Monitor exposure

Self-custody does not protect a position from price movement or liquidation.

06 / EXIT

Close and withdraw deliberately

Use the supported workflow and verify the destination whenever funds move.

SELF-CUSTODY TRADING FAQ

Questions before connecting your wallet.

What is self-custody crypto trading?+

Self-custody trading keeps wallet credentials and transaction authorization with the user. The user is responsible for protecting keys and approving supported interactions.

Does self-custody remove trading risk?+

No. Market, leverage, liquidation, smart-contract, network and infrastructure risks can still apply.

Will HOODBTC ever need my seed phrase?+

No. Do not send your seed phrase or private key to another person to use HOODBTC Trade.

What should I check before signing?+

Verify the domain, network, destination, requested permission and transaction details before approval.
