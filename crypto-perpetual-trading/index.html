CRYPTO PERPETUAL TRADING

PERPS / MARGIN / LEVERAGE / LIQUIDATION

Understand perpetual trading before opening a position.

Crypto perpetual contracts are derivatives that can provide long or short market exposure without a fixed expiry date. They can involve collateral, margin, funding and leverage. These mechanics can increase trading flexibility, but they also increase the speed and severity of potential losses.

PERPETUAL BASICS

Know the mechanics before using leverage.

The exact fields and risk parameters vary by market and connected infrastructure, but several concepts are common across perpetual trading. Review the live market, collateral requirements and position details before submitting an order.

01 / LONG

Long exposure

A long position generally benefits if the referenced market rises, but losses occur if it moves against the position.

02 / SHORT

Short exposure

A short position generally benefits if the referenced market falls, while rising prices can create losses.

03 / MARGIN

Collateral supports the position

Perpetual trading typically requires eligible collateral and margin to keep a leveraged position open.

04 / LEVERAGE

Exposure can exceed collateral

Leverage magnifies both gains and losses. Small market moves can have a large impact on account equity.

05 / FUNDING

Funding can affect holding cost

Many perpetual markets use recurring funding payments or similar mechanisms to help align contract and underlying prices.

06 / LIQUIDATION

Positions can be forcibly closed

If margin falls below required levels, the position may be liquidated according to the market's rules.

PERPETUAL TRADING FAQ

Quick answers.

What is a crypto perpetual contract?+

It is a derivative contract designed to track an underlying market without a fixed expiry date.

Can I lose money quickly with leverage?+

Yes. Leverage increases exposure relative to collateral, so adverse moves can create rapid losses or liquidation.

Does HOODBTC guarantee perpetual market availability?+

No. Supported markets and geographic availability depend on the live trading infrastructure and may change over time.
